**Time.** Posting dates are real but almost all fall within ~2 weeks of the scrape. That supports a time-based hold-out test and a with/without-time ablation, but not demand forecasting or 'emerging skill' trend claims.

**Salary units.** All USD-labelled salaries in this dataset are Indian roles quoting rupee amounts (e.g. a Nagpur trainee at '10,000-15,000 USD PA'); converting them would invent salaries, so they are flagged currency_suspect and excluded. ## 8. Limitations

- Naukri postings are formal-sector, English-language and urban-skewed; the informal economy is invisible here.
- About two weeks of postings: no trend or forecast is claimed.
- ESCO is European; many current tools (cloud platforms, frameworks) have no ESCO skill and stay as raw tags.
- Eligibility (covering a share of a posting's skills) is not hiring; no outcome data exists in this dataset.
- Salary is posted, not realised, and only disclosed on a non-random third of postings.
- The recruiter candidate pool is SYNTHETIC and labelled as such everywhere; it demonstrates ranking, not real-candidate quality.
- Supply is a state-level proxy (AISHE out-turn, PLFS youth unemployment), not field-level graduate counts.
- Gold linking metrics depend on the team's labels; until labelled, thresholds are provisional.
